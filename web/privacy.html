Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. The Simple Rule: Your Data Is Yours

Kineti is your personal assistant. We never sell your personal information, train public models on your private messages, or run third-party advertisements.

2. Real Memory With Instant Deletion

Kineti remembers what you tell it (such as food allergies, preferred airlines, and favorite coffee) so it can help you faster.

If you text "Forget my home address" or "Delete my card details", Kineti immediately creates an irreversible cryptographic tombstone. The data becomes invisible instantly and is permanently scrubbed from disk.

3. Financial Protection

Kineti never spends money without your explicit 2-step confirmation. Every transaction shows the exact merchant, exact items, and final price before you say YES.
