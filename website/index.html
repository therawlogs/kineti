Evidence in motion
The flow

From check to gate, within your workflow.

A clear record of what ran, what changed, and what needs attention before a participating workflow moves forward.

  1. Record the check ↗

    Run a supported test through Kineti and keep evidence of the command and its result.

  2. Notice what changed ↗

    Check the evidence against recorded inputs and rerun when it becomes stale.

  3. Gate the workflow ↗

    Check required evidence and reported spending policy in the CI workflows you choose.

Requirements and important limits

Kineti is proprietary. Registry distribution is retired. Historical releases retain their original license terms.

Kineti only gates workflows that explicitly use it. It does not provide automatic interception or protected writes. Signed records do not prove tests were honest, and reported costs are not provider bills.

MCP integration has been tested on macOS arm64 with Codex 0.163.0-alpha.11.2 and 0.164.0. Other versions and agents, including Cursor and Antigravity, are unverified.
