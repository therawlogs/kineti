Privacy & Data Handling

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

1. Offline Runner & Zero Telemetry

Kineti 1.0.1 runs offline on your organization’s Linux CI runners (such as GitHub Actions) or local developer machines. The native kineti executable sends zero telemetry, makes zero outbound network requests, and does not connect to a Kineti-hosted evaluator, SaaS dashboard, or shared account service.

2. Local State & Signed Audit Artifacts

Project state, test receipts, signing keys, reported spend records under .kineti/, and exported kineti-pr-audit-v1 JSON or Markdown summaries remain strictly on your runner, CI artifact storage, or local filesystem under your retention controls. Keep runtime .kineti/ files out of Git commits (commit only .kineti/gate.json).

3. Credentials & Command Arguments

Receipts and undo logs record exact command argv and do not automatically redact credentials passed on the command line. Keep secrets, API keys, and tokens in protected CI secrets or environment inputs—never in command arguments, labels, fixture files, or test output. Reported spending records operator-entered amounts and does not fetch or verify provider bills.

4. Third-Party Hosts & Static Website

Your Git host, CI runners, coding agents, and model providers process data under their own privacy policies. The static getkineti.com site loads zero third-party analytics, ad trackers, or external CDN scripts. Emails sent to hello@getkineti.com or security@getkineti.com are used solely to respond to your pilot inquiry, support request, or private security report.

5. Contact

Questions about data handling, open-core licensing, or Founding Team Pilot onboarding may be directed to hello@getkineti.com.
